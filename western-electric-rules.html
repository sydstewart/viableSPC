When you look at your chart, keep an eye out for these signals defined by the Western Electric principles:

  • Rule 1: The Outlier
    One single point falls far outside the "normal" bounds (3-sigma).
    Investigator's Action: Check for a specific, singular cause. Don't panic; it’s likely just a one-off event.
  • Rule 2: The Run
    Nine or more points in a row stay on one side of the average line.
    Investigator's Action: The system has shifted. Something changed in the process—investigate what it was.
  • Rule 3: The Trend
    Six or more points in a row are steadily climbing or falling.
    Investigator's Action: You have a "drift." Intervene now to steer the process back before it hits a danger zone.
  • Rule 4: The Shift
    Two out of three consecutive points fall near the outer danger limits (2-sigma).
    Investigator's Action: This is an early warning siren. The process is being pushed to its limit—find the lever and stabilize it.

The Golden Rule

If your data does not show these patterns, the system is "in control." Any movement you see is just the natural rhythm of the system. Do not adjust your process. Tampering with a stable system is the fastest way to introduce unwanted chaos.
