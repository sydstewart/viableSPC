Checking your data

Every file is checked automatically. When you load a file into the StepChange Analyzer, it is checked before anything is analysed, using exactly the same rules as the analysis itself. (This replaces the separate Data Validator.)

What the data check shows

  • How your numbers and dates will be read (UK or European numbers; day-first or month-first dates), and a preview of the columns you have chosen.
  • Rows that will be left out, and why: blank values, text, impossible dates.
  • Missing periods, and where they are. You choose whether they mean no activity (entered as 0) or no data.
  • Dates with more than one row, and censored values such as <5. You choose how to treat them.
  • Zero values to check, and series too short to give strong evidence.

If a file cannot be read one way, for example it mixes day-first and month-first dates, the analyser stops and shows example rows, so nothing is analysed under the wrong assumption.
